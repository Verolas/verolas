# ADR 000: Template

- Date: YYYY-MM-DD
- Deciders: list of names

> Status values: proposed, accepted, superseded by ADR XXX, deprecated.

## Context

What is the problem we are solving. What constraints, requirements, regulatory rules, or customer commitments shape the decision. State what is in scope and what is out of scope.

## Options considered

For each option, capture:

- One sentence description
- Pros (specific, ideally measurable)
- Cons (specific, ideally measurable)
- Cost implications (time, money, vendor risk)
- Reversibility (one way, two way, or hybrid)

Aim for at least two real options. "Do nothing" counts and is often the right baseline.

### Option A: ...

### Option B: ...

## Decision

State the chosen option in one sentence. Reference it by name.

## Consequences

What changes as a result. List positive and negative consequences. Call out:

- New work created (link to issue)
- Work avoided
- New risks introduced
- Migration path if this supersedes a prior decision

## Compliance and audit notes

If the decision affects the EU AI Act high risk obligations, professional liability, data residency, or any regulated standard (EN, DIN, ASCE, ACI, AISC), note that here with the specific clause or article references.
